IronMut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

IronMut is a private tool. It does not run a public website and does not sell accounts.

When connected to TikTok, the tool may receive the operator’s TikTok account identifiers and permission to upload or publish videos to that same account. That access is used only to post IronMut content.

No audience personal data is collected, sold, or shared with advertisers. Tokens are stored only so the operator can publish.

TikTok processes data under TikTok’s own privacy policy when you use TikTok.

Contact: the IronMut operator via the TikTok account linked to this app.
